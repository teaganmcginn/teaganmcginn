About me

For me, marketing is about more than creating content. It’s about building relationships and making a lasting impression.

I love bringing brands to life through creative storytelling, thoughtful strategy and content that builds genuine connections with audiences.

My experience covers both the creative and strategic sides of marketing, from brainstorming campaigns and developing content strategies to filming, editing and analyzing performance. I especially enjoy seeing an idea come to life and understanding what makes it resonate.

I love collaborating, bring positive energy to every project, and look for new ways to help brands stand out while staying true to who they are.

Outside of marketing, you’ll usually find me exploring a new coffee shop, trying a new recipe, or spending time with family and friends.
